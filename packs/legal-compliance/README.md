# Legal and compliance office pack

Check licences of dependencies and assets and keep the record. Drafts only; not legal advice.

The pack adds one office with 3 members of its own. Give the lead (`legal-lead`) a goal in plain words.

## Members

| Id | Title | What they do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `legal-lead` | Legal Lead | Leads the legal and compliance office: turns a licence question into checks for the team by name and reports what a lawyer should confirm |
| `licence-compatibility-checker` | Licence Compatibility Checker | Compares the licences in a project with the licence it ships under and lists conflicts |
| `asset-licence-recorder` | Asset Licence Recorder | Records the name, author, source, licence and date of every shipped asset in the project record |

## Sample tasks

1. **Record the licences of three invented assets** (for `asset-licence-recorder`): For the three invented asset entries I name, write the licence record and mark any missing field.
2. **Compare invented dependency licences** (for `licence-compatibility-checker`): Compare the invented licence list I name with the licence the project ships under and list possible conflicts.

## Limits

- Members prepare drafts and reports. They never send, post, pay or publish; Arsenale asks before any such step.
- A pack can only make the rules stricter.
